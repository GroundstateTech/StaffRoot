# Project Governance

StaffRoot is a Groundstate Technology LLC project distributed under the repository's stated license. The official repository is stewarded by Groundstate Technology LLC and designated maintainers.

## Official stewardship

Maintainers may review, request changes, merge, defer, or decline contributions based on project scope, payroll/HR correctness, privacy, security, maintainability, compatibility, licensing, and long-term direction. Public source availability does not require the official project to accept every proposed change.

## Ownership and licensing

Groundstate Technology LLC retains ownership of Groundstate-created code, documentation, project branding, and other Company-created material, subject to rights granted by the repository license. Contributors retain copyright in their contributions unless a separate written assignment applies.

Submitting a contribution does not create employment, partnership, agency, membership interests, equity, compensation, royalties, or ownership in Groundstate Technology LLC.

## Payroll, HR, and privacy sensitivity

StaffRoot handles categories of information that may be highly sensitive in real deployments. Public repositories, issues, test fixtures, screenshots, and pull requests must not contain real Social Security numbers, payroll records, employee medical information, authentication secrets, private addresses, banking details, tax identifiers, or other confidential personnel information.

Contributors should use synthetic test data. Features involving payroll calculations, tax forms, withholding, benefits, employment law, identity, or financial integrations must clearly distinguish software functionality from legal, tax, payroll, or regulatory certification.

## Groundstate Control Center integration

StaffRoot may integrate with Groundstate's central identity and authorization systems. Such integration must preserve least-privilege access, auditable role boundaries, and separation between HR/payroll records and unrelated application data.

## Forks and branding

Forking and redistribution are governed by the repository license. Materially modified distributions should use distinct branding and must not falsely represent themselves as official Groundstate or StaffRoot releases. See `BRAND_POLICY.md`.

## Stewardship objective

The official project should remain coherent, privacy-conscious, secure-by-default, testable, documented, and suitable for responsible development without implying that prototype software itself guarantees payroll, tax, employment-law, or regulatory compliance.
